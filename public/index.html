CultureRisk
Audience coverage for film & television Every finding sourced from Qloo's taste graph

A greenlight commits tens of millions on a sentence: "it's X meets Y." Nobody checks whether X's audience and Y's audience are the same people, whether the script attracts them, or which brands, artists and places they love. CultureRisk sends a research agent into Qloo's taste graph to find out, then files coverage you can take into the room.
